# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Flowlyra to be a welcoming, collaborative, and technically constructive open-source community.

Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to treat one another with respect regardless of experience level, background, identity, role, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving technical feedback respectfully
- assuming good intent while discussing mistakes
- keeping criticism focused on code, design, or ideas rather than people
- helping new contributors understand project conventions
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting errors openly
- respecting privacy and confidential information
- accepting maintainers' decisions after reasonable discussion

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory remarks
- personal attacks or sustained hostile behavior
- publishing another person's private information without permission
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention
- deliberate disruption of project discussions
- spam, scams, or malicious contributions
- knowingly submitting secrets, private source code, or confidential data

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in Flowlyra project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, community channels, and project-related public interactions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these expectations. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent participation restrictions.

If you need to report conduct that should not be discussed publicly, contact the project maintainer privately through an appropriate GitHub contact method.

Maintainers should handle reports fairly, discreetly, and with respect for the privacy and safety of everyone involved.
